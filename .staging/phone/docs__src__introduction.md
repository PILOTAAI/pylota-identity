## 1. "## What you get"

- **Phone numbers for agents** (behind a flag). A UK mobile or a US local number on an identity, registered in
  your business's own name, bought by an agent in one call inside a budget a person sets, after a one-time
  set-up per country. SMS in and out with consent, caps and US 10DLC handled; verification codes kept from the
  model when the vault fills them; calls through Vapi with an AI disclosure; and a person decides anything that
  costs money or lets a number go ([Phone numbers](guides/phone.md)).
